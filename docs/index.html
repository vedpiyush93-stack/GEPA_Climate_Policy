How these labels were made

1Expert labelsClimate Policy Radar's domain experts marked each passage: does it hold a net zero target, an emission reduction target, or another target with a number and a deadline?
2Split by countryThe countries were split into five groups. Each group was held out once, so a model never sees the country it is judging.
3GEPA coachingOn the other four groups, GEPA had Claude Sonnet read Claude Haiku's mistakes and rewrite its instructions, starting from the experts' own written definition.
4Every passageEach fold's coached Haiku then labelled every passage of its held-out countries and explained why. That is what you are browsing.
Data: Climate Policy Radar, national-climate-targets (CC BY 4.0; Juhasz et al. 2024, arXiv:2404.02822). Method: GEPA (Agrawal et al., International Conference on Learning Representations 2026, arXiv:2507.19457). Code, data and cached model responses: GitHub.
